## **2. Carousel: "3 Key Insights for the Mature Leader"**

### **Slide Script (5 Slides)**

*   **Slide 1: Title**
    *   **Main Text:** 3 SHIFTS FOR THE MATURE LEADER
    *   **Sub-text:** Navigating responsibility and complexity with Constanze Buchheim.
*   **Slide 2: Insight 1 - The Somatic Reframe**
    *   **Content:** We often carry leadership as a physical burden on our shoulders, a weight that limits our movement. Constanze Buchheim suggests a radical somatic shift: seeing responsibility not as a weight pressing down, but as the act of "giving your shoes to the snow." It is the empowered ability to respond to reality and pave a stable path for others to follow safely.
*   **Slide 3: Insight 2 - The Turkey Phenomenon**
    *   **Content:** Relying solely on historical data is a dangerous "Turkey Phenomenon." Like a bird expecting food until Thanksgiving Day arrives, data cannot predict systemic disruptions or cultural shifts. Mature leaders must integrate "atmospheric intelligence"—the whole-body capacity to sense emotional dynamics and intuitive signals that spreadsheets inevitably ignore.
*   **Slide 4: Insight 3 - The Three Rooms**
    *   **Content:** To lead with clarity and without burnout, we must respect the "three rooms": my internal space, your internal space, and the shared relationship space. We are responsible for the integrity of what we put into the middle, but never for how the other person chooses to process or react to it within their own room. True maturity is knowing where you end and others begin.
*   **Slide 5: Outro**
    *   **Main Text:** READY FOR THE INNER PIONEERS?
