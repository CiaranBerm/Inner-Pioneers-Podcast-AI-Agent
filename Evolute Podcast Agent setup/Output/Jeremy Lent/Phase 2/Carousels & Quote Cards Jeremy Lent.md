### **Slide 2: Insight 1**
**The "Wealth Pump" is Not a Glitch**
Our global crisis isn't a systemic failure, but the logical result of a 500-year-old mechanistic worldview that treats the living Earth as a machine for extraction. By recognizing this "Cultural Hegemony," we can finally see the "Wealth Pump" that systematically sucks value from life into the hands of a tiny elite, and begin the work of dismantling it.

---

### **Slide 3: Insight 2**
**Reclaiming Eudaimonia**
We are often trapped on a "hedonic treadmill" of perpetual dissatisfaction, fueled by a system that requires constant consumption to survive. Reclaiming "eudaimonia"—the ancient concept of internal flourishing of our true nature—is not just a personal choice, but a radical act of resistance and the fundamental building block of an ecological civilization.

---

### **Slide 4: Insight 3**
**Islands of Coherence**
Global transformation doesn't require reinventing the wheel from scratch. From the Mondragon cooperatives to the shared knowledge of Wikipedia, the "Islands of Coherence" are already flourishing. These models prove that a life-affirming economy based on the commons is not only possible but is already being built by those who choose to step out of the extraction machine.
