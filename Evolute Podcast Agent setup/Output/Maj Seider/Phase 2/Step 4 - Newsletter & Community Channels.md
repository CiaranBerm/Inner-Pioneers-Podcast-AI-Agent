# Step 4: Newsletter & Community Channels - Maj Seider

## 1. Launch Newsletter

**Subject:** Can a leader lead without making a sound?

Dear fellow explorer,

We are often taught that leadership is a solo performance—a hero at the front of the room, directing the flow of results through sheer force of will. But what if the most powerful thing a leader can do is stop making a sound?

In our latest episode of The Inner Pioneers Podcast, we sit down with Maj Seider, Partner at Lead Forward, to explore the profound maturation process that takes an executive from technical management to systemic stewardship. Maj brings a unique perspective, forged in the belly of global corporate giants like Vestas and GfK, and refined through her commitment to B-Corp values and open-hearted pragmatism.

She shares a metaphor that has stayed with us: the conductor. A conductor’s influence isn't found in their own noise, but in their ability to hold the space for a collective masterpiece. Maj argues that this shift from individual intelligence (IQ) to collective intelligence (WeQ) is the defining maturation process for today's pioneers. It requires us to decenter the ego and trust the social system to unfold its own potential.

We dive deep into the "Speed Paradox"—the systemic necessity of slowing down to achieve genuine velocity—and why our "burned finger" moments are actually the raw materials for our greatest resilience. Maj’s approach is sharp, playful, and deeply human, offering a path forward for anyone feeling the weight of the traditional "hero" leadership model in a world of mounting complexity.

We invite you to step into this "slow space" with us and listen to the full conversation.

Explore the episode with Maj Seider here: https://evolute-institute.com/podcast/maj-seider?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=maj_seider_launch

With warmth and curiosity,
Dmitrij & the Evolute Team
