## **1. LAUNCH NEWSLETTER**

**Subject:** Beyond Cleverness: A Radical Invitation from Philip Shepherd

Dear Fellow Explorers,

We are living in the cleverest culture in human history. We have mastered abstraction, data, and the art of "thinking through" our problems. And yet, many of us feel a persistent sense of disconnection—a feeling that despite our cleverness, we have forgotten the fundamental intelligence of how to live.

In our latest episode of The Inner Pioneers Podcast, we are joined by Philip Shepherd, the visionary founder of The Embodied Present Process and author of Radical Wholeness. Philip uncovers what he calls the "lesion" in Western culture: a deep-seated rift between our thinking minds and our felt being. He argues that our 10,000-year "ascent" into the head has left us "whole-blind," severed from the oceanic intelligence of the body that processes a billion times more information than our conscious thoughts ever could.

This conversation is a radical invitation to "lower your consciousness." Philip challenges the modern obsession with self-achieved independence—labeling it a "fantasy" that fuels our anxiety—and points us instead toward the security found in relationship and somatic grounding. By reclaiming the pelvic bowl as a center of inclusive, relational intelligence, we can move from a tyrannical mode of control to a heroic mode of attunement with the present.

We found this talk to be one of the most sobering and liberating explorations we’ve ever hosted. It is a roadmap for anyone navigating the friction of modern life and seeking a deeper, more harmonious way of being in the world.

You can listen to the full conversation with Philip Shepherd here: [Direct Link to Episode]

We look forward to hearing how these insights land with you.

With warmth and curiosity,

## **2. WHATSAPP TRIBE MESSAGE**

Hi everyone, Dmitrij here. I wanted to share something deeply personal that’s been sitting with me since our recent recording. For years, I’ve been chasing the idea of "raising my consciousness"—seeking higher states and more elevated ways of thinking. But our latest guest, Philip Shepherd, completely flipped that script for me. He told me to do the exact opposite: to lower it.

Philip talks about the "lesion" between our heads and our bodies—a cultural wound that leaves us trapped in a "castle" of cleverness while we lose the touch of the actual world. One idea that really landed was his rewrite of Descartes: "I relate, therefore I am." It’s such a simple yet profound shift away from the myth of independence that keeps so many of us feeling isolated and anxious.

I’ve been practicing his suggestion of initiating the breath from the pelvic floor, and it’s been a revelation in finding a sense of security that isn't contingent on external success. I wanted to share an early link to the episode with this tribe first. It’s a deep one, and I think it speaks directly to the "inner transitions" many of us are navigating right now.
