# The Evolute Podcast Promotion Plan (Unified Workflow)

This document outlines our strategy for episode launches. All content generation must strictly follow the **5-Step Workflow** and the **Podcast Promotion Workflow Syntax.csv**.

## Phase 0: The Day Before Launch (Tuesday)
*Anticipation and network activation.*

### Throughout the Day: Network Activation (Step 4)
*   **WhatsApp Tribe:** Deeply human and peer-to-peer (800–1,000 characters).
*   **Guest Favour Emails:** Humble, appreciative, and respectful (Min 1,000 characters).

## Phase 1: Launch Day (Wednesday)
*Maximum visibility within the first 24 hours.*

### The Morning Push (9:00 AM)
*   **The Main Upload (Step 1 & 4):** Full video goes live. Description follows the Expert Template in Step 1.
*   **LinkedIn Takeover (Step 3):**
    *   *Host’s Main Post:* Business focused. Big picture and strategy. (1,200–3,000 characters).
    *   *Co-Founder Post:* Philosophical and personal. Focus on "the why." (1,000–3,000 characters).
*   **SFV Clip 1 (Step 1):** Post across IG, YT, and TikTok (500–1,200 chars).
*   **Instagram Post Announcement (Step 4):** Launch announcement caption (900–1,050 characters).

### The Evening Push (6:00 PM)
*   **LinkedIn Repost (Step 3):** Evolute Page reshares Dmitrij's post with context (700–3,000 characters).
*   **The Newsletter (Step 4):** Launch broadcast (Min 1,200 characters).
