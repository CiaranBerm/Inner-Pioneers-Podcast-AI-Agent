# THE DEFINITIVE EVOLUTE PODCAST PROMOTION AI WORKFLOW

This master blueprint directs the AI to transform a podcast transcript into a comprehensive promotional package. To ensure maximum "Vigor & Detail," the process is split into four distinct steps.

## **Execution Strategy**
The AI must complete these steps sequentially. Do not combine them. Focus on one step at a time to maintain the **"Martha Beck Level of Detail."**

## **Universal Quality Standards**
1.  **The 4-Step Narrative Arc:** Hook → Guest Context → Technical Insight → Invitational CTA.
2.  **The "Fellow Explorer" Tone:** Epistemic humility, intellectually rigorous, deeply human.
3.  **Strict Character Minimums:** Refer to each step's specific constraints (derived from the `Podcast Promotion Workflow Syntax.csv`).
4.  **UTM Tracking:** Apply the standard tracking parameters to all links as defined in Step 4.
5.  **Automatic Organization (MANDATORY):** All Step 1–4 outputs must be saved directly into the pre-existing `Output/[Guest Name]/Phase 2/` directory created during Phase 1.
